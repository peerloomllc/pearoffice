PearOffice v1.1.7

New Features
  - Open files from other apps on Android. Tap a spreadsheet, document, presentation or PDF in Files, your downloads or an email, and PearOffice is offered to open it
  - Share files to PearOffice on Android. Use Share in any app and pick PearOffice to bring the file in
  - Open in PearOffice on iPhone and iPad. Spreadsheets, documents, presentations and PDFs from Files or Mail can now be opened in PearOffice

Improvements
  - On desktop, the update bar now shows as soon as a new version is found

Bug Fixes
  - On desktop, PearOffice could open with "Cannot read properties of null" and no files, devices or name after an earlier copy had closed unexpectedly. It now shuts down fully when it closes, and if your files are ever still in use it tells you plainly what to do

Privacy
  - Scanning a QR code no longer uses Google's scanning library. Codes are read inside the app, and nothing about your scans goes to Google
